An Ecuadorian home, since 2001.

Coastal Ecuadorian cooking

Since 2001 we have cooked the coast of Ecuador with fresh seafood, plenty of lime, pickled onion and recipes passed from family to family. Today we are seven houses across Spain and the United States — still serving encebollado the way we did on day one.
